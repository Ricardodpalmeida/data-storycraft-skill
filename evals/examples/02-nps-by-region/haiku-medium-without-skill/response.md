Here is the QBR chart: monthly NPS for each of the seven regions across 2026, with the Nordics highlighted because it is the one region that moved materially.

**Headline:** Nordics NPS has fallen 22 points since March (46 to 24), while the other six regions stayed within a narrow band through the year.

**What the chart shows**
- Nordics peaked at 46 in March and declined in every month after that, from 43 in April to 24 in December. The slide is steady rather than a single bad month, so it is worth a root-cause look before the review.
- The other regions each moved within a range of about 2.5 to 4 points over the year. Year-end values are UK 44.8, DACH 44.1, Iberia 42.4, Italy 39.3, Benelux 38.3 and France 36.1.

**Assumptions**
- Values are NPS points as given in the file (no weighting, sample sizes or confidence intervals were provided, so none are shown).
- December is the latest full month, so the year-end values are treated as the current position.
- "Since March" uses March as the Nordics peak, since it is the highest value in the series.
- The chart is a single line chart on a fixed 20 to 50 scale so the regions are comparable at a glance.
