Audience:  Product team, especially the owner of the Exports feature
Ask:       Prioritise a fix for Exports and find out why users are dissatisfied
Mechanism: Sent document (chart shared as a PNG)
Big Idea:  Exports is the only feature where more respondents are dissatisfied (41%) than satisfied (38%), so it should be the first satisfaction fix; the other four features have 12% to 22% dissatisfied.

Chart: <workspace>/06-feature-satisfaction/with_skill_haiku/chart.png

The eye lands on the orange segments of the Exports bar and the bold 41% in the right-hand column.

Chart choice: a 100% stacked horizontal bar, the default for a Likert scale. Rows are sorted by share satisfied or very satisfied (Search 70%, Dashboards 62%, Alerts 58%, Sharing 54%, Exports 38%). Negative levels are orange, neutral is grey, positive levels are blue. Only the Exports row carries value labels, and the right-hand column gives total dissatisfied for every feature so the comparison is easy to read.

Assumptions:
- The percentages are of the 1,240 respondents for each feature, and each row sums to 100 (verified).
- "Satisfied" for the comparison means satisfied plus very satisfied, and "dissatisfied" means dissatisfied plus very dissatisfied.
- The team wants a chart for a sent document, so there is no live hover layer.
- The first satisfaction fix is a recommendation drawn from the data, not something the survey alone proves; the survey does not show why Exports scores low.
