The chart is ready for the e-commerce update. It shows mobile's share of checkouts at 41% in 2024 and 68% in 2026, a 27-point rise.

Files in this folder:
- chart.png (1600 x 960 px)
- make_chart.py (Python/matplotlib script that reads the CSV and builds the chart)

Assumptions I made:
- The values are shares of total checkouts, as the column name says, not revenue or order value.
- Only two years are in the file, so I used a two-bar comparison rather than a trend line. A line would imply a path between the points that the data does not show.
- 2025 is absent from the source file and is not estimated. The footnote on the chart says so.
- "Rose 27 points" is 68 minus 41. If the update needs a relative figure, the share grew about 66% (68 / 41), so say which framing you want.
