## Biggest problem

The slide tries to show six things at once (market share, revenue, headcount, and a dual axis), so the one point the director needs, that headcount rose and revenue did not, is buried.

## What was wrong

| Lesson | Issue | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Context | Title "Market Overview 2025" is a topic, and the data runs Q1 2024 to Q4 2025 | No takeaway for sales leadership | Action title: "Adding 17 sales reps has not grown quarterly revenue" |
| Visual | Pie chart for market share; revenue bars and reps line on two y-axes | Pie angles are hard to compare; the dual axis makes the lines look related when the scales create that | Two stacked panels on one x-axis, reps as bars, revenue as a line |
| Visual | Bar chart with the y-axis starting at $4.0M | Small differences look like big swings; revenue looks volatile when it is flat | Zero-based axis for revenue; zero-based bars for reps |
| Clutter | Rainbow colors, shadow, exploded slices, gridlines, two legends | Nothing is emphasized; colors carry no meaning | Grey for context, one blue accent, direct labels, no legend or gridlines |
| Focus | Eight different bar colors | The eye cannot find the point | Revenue line in accent; reps in grey |
| Story | No ask | The meeting ends without a decision | Ask stated in the reply (see below); add to the slide if the director wants it |

## Redesign

Two panels sharing the quarter axis. The top panel shows sales reps as grey bars, labeled 38 and 55. The bottom panel shows quarterly revenue as one blue line on a $0M to $5M axis, labeled $4.1M and $4.2M. The title reads "Adding 17 sales reps has not grown quarterly revenue", with a subtitle giving the figures: reps +45%, revenue +2%. The source is a small grey footnote. The pie is removed.

The eye lands first on the title, then on the flat blue line in the lower panel.

## Assumptions to confirm
- The ask is "decide whether to keep adding reps until revenue per rep is understood". I did not put it on the slide. Tell me the decision you want and I will add it.
- The source line is generic. Replace it with the real source.
- The pie was dropped. If sales leadership wants market share, it belongs on its own slide.
- I used a zero-based revenue axis on purpose. It shows the flat trend honestly; the original axis exaggerated swings.
