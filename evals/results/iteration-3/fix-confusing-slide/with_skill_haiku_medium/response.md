# Revised slide: sales reps vs revenue

**Biggest problem:** the slide tries to make three points at once (market share, revenue, headcount), and the one it should make is hidden in a dual-axis chart where the reader cannot tell whether revenue is flat, rising or falling.

## Context

```
Audience:  Sales leadership at the quarterly business review
Ask:       Pause further sales rep hiring until we understand why headcount is not converting into revenue (assumed, confirm with the requester)
Mechanism: Live presentation, deck circulated afterwards
Big Idea:  Adding 17 sales reps since Q1 2024 has not grown revenue, so we should pause further hiring until we understand why headcount is not converting into sales.
```

## What was wrong

| Lesson | Issue | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Context | Title "Market Overview 2025" is a topic, with no takeaway or ask | The director cannot tell what to decide from the slide | Action title and an explicit ask |
| Visual | Pie chart for market share, plus a bar chart and a line on a secondary y-axis | Pie angles are hard to compare, and the two y-axes let the scales be chosen so the lines appear to cross | Two stacked line panels sharing one quarter axis |
| Visual | Revenue bars start at $4.0M, not zero | The Q4 24 peak of $4.6M looks several times larger than the $4.1M start, which is a distortion | Zero baseline in both panels |
| Clutter | 3D import, pie shadow and explode, dashed gridlines, two legends, rotated x-labels, eight bar colors | Every element competes with the message | All removed |
| Focus | Eight rainbow colors and red/green pairs on equal weight | Nothing stands out, and red/green is hard for colorblind readers | Reps in neutral grey, revenue as the single blue accent, direct labels |
| Story | Market share has no link to the point being made | It pulls attention to the wrong message | Dropped from this slide; use a separate slide if the director needs it |

## Redesign

Two stacked line charts share one quarter axis, both starting at zero. The top panel shows sales reps rising from 38 to 55 (up 45%). The bottom panel shows quarterly revenue, which ends 2025 at $4.2M against $4.1M in Q1 24. Every point is labelled directly, the peak is called out, and the source is in a small grey footnote. The eye lands first on the title, then on the flat blue revenue line, which is the message.
